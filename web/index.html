Sprinto outreach

Give it company domains. For each one it decides whether the company fits Sprinto's ICP (SaaS, Series A–C, under compliance pressure), finds the right person, and writes a personal cold email that is sent only to our own inbox. Every decision is explained.

The 7 steps

1 ScrapeRead the site, strip clutter
2 ExtractFacts + quotes (Gemini)
3 QualifyLoop for missing facts, score /100
4 ContactApollo / Hunter.io, role by size
5 Pain pointsTwo, each with evidence
6 DraftBy name, specific hook, checked
7 SendTo our own inbox only

Run it

One company domain per line. Each takes about a minute.
